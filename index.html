What these comparisons can tell us

County vote shares describe ballots cast in 2024. They do not measure the political preferences of every resident, or explain why a facility was sited there. Population-weighted vote shares apply each county’s observed vote share to its resident population as a geographic index; they are not estimated votes.

Resident population comes directly from the Census Bureau’s July 1, 2024 estimates. Dividing votes by a turnout rate would estimate an electorate only if the rate’s denominator matched; it would not recover children or other ineligible residents. Capacity per resident is a siting intensity measure, not measured electricity use or environmental harm.

Sources & methodology

A snapshot, not a census of facilities

The local September 2026 Brockovich snapshot contains 474 tracked projects. Coverage may be selective. Operational, construction, permitted, proposed and cancelled records are distinct stages—not a time series. Announced MW may describe eventual campus capacity.

Comparable denominators

Unknown MW and investment are excluded from reported sums and their coverage is shown. Per-resident capacity uses only counties with population and at least one selected facility reporting MW; unknown facility capacity is not imputed. Counties with several projects contribute their population once.

Political categories

Strong means the winning candidate received at least 65% of all votes; lean means a win below 65%. Third-party votes remain a separate gray share. A presidential county winner does not establish local political control.

Join limitations

Original election matches use state and normalized county names, then attach FIPS codes; coordinates have not been independently checked. North Slope’s manually entered election tally remains unverified. New London County has no matching geography in the Census 2024 county file and is excluded from population metrics.
